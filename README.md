## The three passes, in order

**1 · Fact check** (`fact-check-corrections.md`)
Found the volume thesis was confounded with time — correlation drops to −0.13 within 2026, and
"fewer videos = more total views" was backwards. Also corrected Amy Bouzaglo's conviction, a
trafficking MDL that was never consolidated, an unverifiable violation count, and the sequel
decay figure. Later retracted the "zero results on YouTube" novelty claims: only the elevator
topic survives.

**2 · JD audit** (`jd-alignment-audit.md`, Part 1)
The JD had never been in the repo — prior work ran on a paraphrase. Found the AD2 diagnosis
contradicted the brief, Hotels was mis-routed, two sub-questions were unanswered, and Patreon,
monetization and level-transition retention had no coverage at all.

**3 · Premise doc** (`jd-alignment-audit.md`, Part 2)
Overrode the JD's channel summaries and corrected three of Part 1's own findings. Then surfaced
the big ones: the format requires **35–50 cases** and all three pitches carried 16–26; true
crime without a footage anchor is rejected network-wide, which kills Hotels outright; and the
test explicitly measures whether you can propose the **next** format, which nothing addressed.

---

## Where the answer landed

**Q1 —** The decline is not the titles (a constant through the peak) and not volume (confounded
with time). It is that the **45–60 minute, 35–50 case requirement took over topic selection**:
to fill fifty filmed segments you need a topic broad enough that nobody has pre-existing morbid
curiosity about it. That is the thing being protected for the wrong reasons.

**Q2 —** Three pitches at full spec:

| Channel | Topic | Cases | Runtime | Already taken? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abyssal Observer | Kitchen Nightmares | 42 (one restaurant each) | 50 min | Yes — Bartolozzi 3.93M. Beaten on structure, not novelty. |
| Abyssal Detective | Structural Collapses | 44 | 55 min | **No.** Genuinely open. |
| Abyssal Detective 2 | Elevator & Escalator Incidents | 40 | 48 min | **No.** Zero mentions across all 330 videos. |

Plus a companion format — **one case, seven levels, 25–35 minutes** — which is the other half of
what the test measures.
