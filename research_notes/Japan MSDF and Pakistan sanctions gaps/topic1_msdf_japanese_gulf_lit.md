## Q1. Has Japanese-language scholarship analysed the decision in depth (journals, NIDS, JIIA, IDE-JETRO, IEEJ, etc.)?

### Takeaway
Searches surfaced no peer-reviewed Japanese journal article (国際安全保障, 国際政治, 中東研究, 国際問題, 防衛研究所紀要) dedicated to the 2019 decision, and none using process tracing (政策決定過程). What does exist is (a) Diet research-office briefs, (b) short think-tank essays (Sasakawa Peace Foundation, Canon Institute for Global Studies, JSIL expert comment), (c) passing treatment in the MSDF Command and Staff College journal 海幹校戦略研究, and (d) a large body of op-eds and journalism. This suggests the gap for an English-language process-tracing study is real, but it is not confirmed, because CiNii/J-STAGE could not be searched directly.

### Cited Findings
- **Sangiin research office, 立法と調査 No.427 (Sept 2020), p.122ff, 「中東地域への自衛隊派遣とイージス・アショアの配備断念」** ("The SDF dispatch to the Middle East and the cancellation of Aegis Ashore deployment"). It is a legislative-research survey, not an analytical article. Per snippets it sets the June 2019 attacks on Japan-related tankers as the background; records the NSC meeting and Cabinet decision of 27 Dec 2019; notes that the June 2020 cabinet decision on the plan's modification explicitly states that "Japan does not participate in any specific framework for navigation safety in the Middle East, and the SDF's information-gathering activity is Japan's own initiative" (「我が国は中東地域の航行の安全に係る特定の枠組みには参加せず、自衛隊の情報収集活動は我が国独自の取組として行うものである」); and reports Diet exchanges on whether using 防衛省設置法第4条第1項第18号 sets a precedent for dispatch "anytime, anywhere". ### Inferences
- The Japanese record is rich in legal-basis debate (the 設置法4条 "調査・研究" controversy) and in description, but thin on explaining why Tokyo chose an independent mission over IMSC (alliance management vs. Iran ties vs. legal constraint vs. Kantei leadership). On the evidence found, an English-language process-tracing study would still fill a gap. It should, however, engage the Diet-research briefs and Nishida (2020) as the nearest existing work.

## Q2. Do NDL / House of Councillors legislative research papers document the decision process?

### Takeaway
Yes, for the House of Councillors: 立法と調査 No.427 (Sept 2020), and apparently No.421 (Feb 2020), document the timeline, legal basis and Diet debate. No NDL 調査と情報 or レファレンス item on this dispatch appeared in any search, so its existence is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- 立法と調査 No.427 covers the timeline (June 2019 attacks, Dec 27 2019 NSC and cabinet decision), the "independent initiative" wording in the June 2020 cabinet decision, and the Diet Q&A on 設置法4条1項18号 as precedent. — [Sangiin PDF](https://www.sangiin.go.jp/japanese/annai/chousa/rippou_chousa/backnumber/2020pdf/20200911122.pdf)
- House of Representatives written questions (質問主意書) give primary-adjacent records of the legal-basis dispute:
  - 「自衛隊の中東海域への派遣の法的根拠に関する質問主意書」 ("Written question on the legal basis for dispatching the SDF to Middle East waters", 第201回国会 No.9). It argues that using 設置法4条1項18号 violates 設置法5条 and asks whether special legislation is needed. — [Shugiin a201009](https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_shitsumon.nsf/html/shitsumon/a201009.htm)
  - A second question (No.171) asks about early withdrawal under COVID-19 and about the dispatch basis. — [Shugiin a201171](https://www.shugiin.go.jp/internet/itdb_shitsumon.nsf/html/shitsumon/a201171.htm)
- Three NDL-oriented searches returned no 調査と情報 / レファレンス hit. — [search results incl. trafficnews](https://trafficnews.jp/post/90753)

### Inferences
- The Sangiin briefs plus the Shugiin written questions (with Cabinet answers) plus kokkai.ndl.go.jp committee minutes (Jan–Feb 2020 budget committees, 安全保障委員会, 外交防衛委員会) make a strong primary-adjacent spine for a process-tracing timeline.

## Q3. What primary sources exist?

### Takeaway
There is an abundant official record (MOD portal, Defense White Papers 2020–2023, Cabinet Office ocean-policy annual report, MOFA press conferences and Abe–Rouhani readouts). Contemporaneous press (Nikkei, Jiji, Japan Times/Kyodo, Bloomberg) traces the June–Dec 2019 deliberation, including the 18 Oct 2019 NSC instruction. As of Nov 2026 the mission is still running: it was extended again on 6 Nov 2026 amid a 2026 US/Israel–Iran war and Hormuz crisis.

### Cited Findings
**Official / government**
- MOD portal 「中東地域における日本関係船舶の安全確保に関する政府の取組」 ("Government efforts to ensure the safety of Japan-related vessels in the Middle East"). ## Q5. Any Japanese work using process tracing / decision-making analysis (政策決定過程) for this case?

### Takeaway
